How this cookie price is worked out

Cost per dozen = ingredients + (hours × hourly rate) + packaging. Price per dozen = cost ÷ (1 − margin), and price per cookie = price per dozen ÷ 12. Example: $9 ingredients, 2 hours at $15, and $3 packaging cost $42 a dozen. At a 20% margin that's $52.50 a dozen, or $4.38 a cookie.
