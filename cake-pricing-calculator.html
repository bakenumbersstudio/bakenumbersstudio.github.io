How this cake price is worked out

Cost = ingredients + (hours × hourly rate) + packaging. Price = cost ÷ (1 − margin). Example: $18 ingredients, 3 hours at $15, and $4 packaging cost $67. At a 20% margin the price is $83.75, which leaves $16.75 profit.
